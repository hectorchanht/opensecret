What it is呢個係咩

OpenSecret opens the beings and phenomena every culture whispered about, with RealUFO discipline: frame-exact evidence standards, source-first, no mockery of witnesses, no credulity for clicks. The dragon dossier comes first — in the age of 100x zoom in every pocket, the absence of one clear dragon photograph is itself the central fact to explain. Every dossier is labelled: historical records, candidate explanations, hypotheses — hypothesis never wears fact's clothes.

OpenSecret 用 RealUFO 嘅證據紀律，打開每個文化都細聲講過嘅存在同現象：逐格證據標準、來源優先、唔嘲笑目擊者、唔為點擊而輕信。第一份檔案係龍——喺人人袋入面都有 100 倍變焦嘅年代，一張清晰龍相都冇，呢個「冇」本身就係要解釋嘅核心事實。每份檔案都有標籤：歷史記錄、候選解釋、假說——假說永遠唔會扮事實。

Dawn doctrine: 見者知，知者行 — secrets published openly don't matter, because the one who DOES it wins.

天光宗法則：見者知，知者行——秘密公開咗都唔緊要，因為贏嘅係真正去做嗰個人。
